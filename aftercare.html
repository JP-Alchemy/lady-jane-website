Aftercare

The healing ritual.

Your tattoo is a living work of art — and the way you care for it will shape how it looks for years to come. Aftercare is not just maintenance; it is the final stage of the artistic process.

The unveiling The cleansing The anointing What to avoid Tips & tricks When to contact me
Day 1 · The first 2 hours

The unveiling

Gently remove the protective wrap (nylon bandage) about 2 hours after your session. If it clings, ease it away with lukewarm water — never force it.

The moment the ink meets the air, the relic begins to awaken.

Days 1 to 2

The cleansing ritual

  • With clean hands, gently wash the tattoo with unscented baby soap or antibacterial soap and lukewarm water.
  • No scrubbing — your tattoo is not a dish, nor is it a battlefield.
  • Pat it dry, or let it air-dry for 15–20 minutes.
  • Repeat this cleansing 3 times a day for the first 48 hours.

Water purifies the path. Be gentle, and let it breathe.

Weeks 1 to 4

The anointing

  • Once your tattoo has fully dried and stopped oozing (after ~48 hours), apply a thin layer of tattoo aftercare balm (e.g. Hustle Butter, Bepanthen).
  • Always cleanse before applying.
  • Apply 3–4 times daily, for at least 3–4 weeks.
  • Keep the area uncovered when possible — let it breathe and integrate.

Salve of restoration. Not too much, not too little.

For at least 4 weeks

What to avoid

No sun

UV rays break down pigment, causing fading and blurring — especially while the skin is healing. Keep it covered, and use sunscreen once fully healed.

No heavy exercise

Heat, sweat and friction irritate fresh skin and can push out pigment. Avoid intense exercise and stretching for 7–10 days; light walking is fine.

No swimming, sauna or humid, salty air

Excess moisture, chemicals and salt water soften the healing layer and can cause ink loss, irritation and patchy spots.

No scratching or picking

Let scabs and dry skin shed on their own. If it sticks to fabric, rinse the material with lukewarm water in the shower until it releases.

No heavy oils or over-moisturising

Thick layers clog pores and trap bacteria. Two fingertips of balm is enough, even on larger pieces — hydrated, never shiny.

Small rituals that help

Tips & tricks

  • Keep it cool — if it feels hot or tight, rinse gently with lukewarm water to reset the skin.
  • Wet wipes when you’re out — alcohol-free, to clean away sweat or dust.
  • Fridge trick — store your lotion in the fridge for a soothing, cooling application.
  • Loose clothing — soft fabrics let the tattoo breathe and stop it sticking.
  • Sleep cool — lighter bedding and a cooler room prevent sweating and sticking at night.
  • Stay hydrated — drinking plenty of water helps your skin heal from the inside.
