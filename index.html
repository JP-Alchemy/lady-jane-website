Meet the tattoo artist

My name is Anneline Yaish.

I am a mystic relic symbolism artist.

Through sacred ink, I create personalised journeys etched in skin — windows into the soul, talismans of memory, emotion and power. My work is more than tattooing; it is resonance embodied.

I guide lost adventurers, forgotten rebels and seekers of meaning through transformational art rooted in ancient cultures, mythologies and mysteries. Each design is a grimoire, a scroll, a relic — crafted uniquely to mirror your path, your pain, your purpose.

Before we begin

Questions, answered.

What tattoo styles do you work in?

Custom, story-driven tattoos in fine line, sketch illustration, vintage engraving and woodcut style, sacred geometry, symbolic and mythological motifs, blackwork realism and hand-poke. Every design is created just for you.

Do I need to know exactly what I want?

No. Many clients come with a feeling, an idea or a symbolic intention. We develop the story together — that’s what the consultation is for.

Can we start with an online consultation?

Yes. A 15-minute online consultation is available for tattoos, and a separate one for art & design commissions. Choose “Online consultation” in the form above or message me on WhatsApp.

How is pricing decided?

By design complexity, size and the length of your session. Every detail is explained during consultation, before anything is booked.

Is a deposit required?

Yes — bookings require a non-refundable deposit that goes toward your final price. You may reschedule once, with at least 24 hours’ notice.

Where is the studio?

On Sheinkin Street (Sheinkin St 4) in central Tel Aviv-Yafo. Sessions are by appointment only — send a request above or message me on WhatsApp to book.

How should I prepare for my session?

Eat beforehand, bring water and snacks, and come with clean skin and comfortable clothes. No alcohol, and skip heavy gym sessions right before. If you’re nervous, tell me — I’m here to help.

Anything I should tell you in advance?

Please share any allergies or medical conditions, and let me know if you’re pregnant or breastfeeding. Diabetic clients will need a clearance letter from their doctor. You must be 18+ with ID.
